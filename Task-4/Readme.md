# Sales Performance & Data Visualization

## 📊 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** created using **Microsoft Power BI**. The project uses the **Sample Superstore** dataset to transform raw business data into meaningful visual insights through data visualization and storytelling.

The report follows a simple story structure:

**Context → Problem → Evidence → Recommendation**

The dashboard contains multiple pages covering overall sales and profit performance, sales trends, category and sub-category profitability, regional performance, and key recommendations.

---

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall sales, profit, and quantity performance.
- Understand sales trends over time.
- Compare sales performance across product categories.
- Analyze profit performance across categories and sub-categories.
- Compare sales and profit across regions.
- Identify important business insights from the visualizations.
- Provide recommendations based on the observed data.

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard creation and data visualization
- **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset/source data
- **DAX** – Measures and calculations
- **Data Visualization & Storytelling** – Presenting insights clearly

# 📄 Dashboard Pages

## 1️⃣ Sales & Profit Performance Overview

This page provides a high-level overview of business performance.

### Key Metrics

- **Total Profit:** 286.40K

### Visualization

**Sales by Category**

The chart compares sales across:

- Technology
- Furniture

## 2️⃣ Sales Trend Over Time

This page analyzes how sales change over time.

## 5️⃣ Key Insights & Recommendations

This page summarizes the major findings from the dashboard.

### 💡 Insight 1 — Sales Performance

Technology has the highest sales among the three product categories.

### 💡 Insight 2 — Profitability

Profitability varies across product categories and sub-categories.

### 💡 Insight 3 — Regional Performance

Sales and profit contributions differ across regions.
